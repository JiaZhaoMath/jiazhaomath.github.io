Energy-stable Runge-Kutta schemes for gradient flow models using the energy quadratization approach

Y. Gong and J. Zhao*

Applied Mathematics Letters, 94, 224-231, 2019.

Publisher (DOI)Google Scholar

Abstract

In this letter, we present a novel class of arbitrarily high-order and unconditionally energy-stable algorithms for gradient flow models by combining the energy quadratization (EQ) technique and a specific class of Runge-Kutta (RK) methods, which is named the EQRK schemes. First of all, we introduce auxiliary variables to transform the original model into an equivalent system, with he transformed free energy a quadratic functional with respect to the new variables and the modified energy dissipative law is conserved. Then a special class of RK methods is employed for the reformulated system to arrive at structure-preserving time-discrete schemes. Along with rigorous proofs, numerical experiments are presented to demonstrate the accuracy and unconditionally energy-stability of the EQRK schemes.
